DBT Schemes of SC Welfare Department 1) Scheme: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Merit Award Eligibility: i) Domicile of Tripura. ii) Belongs to SC community. iii) Studying in Class VI to XII in any school within Tripura. iv) Secured at least 60% marks while promoting to next class. Benefits: Sl. No. Studying in Class Award Amount (Rs.) 1 VI 400 2 VII 400 3 VIII 400 4 IX 700 5 X 1500 6 XII 1500 Frequency: one time per year. Proposed Workflow: i) Maker: Student or SDWO ii) Checker: DWO iii) Approver: SC Welfare
