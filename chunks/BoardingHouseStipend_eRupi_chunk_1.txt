Department: Tribal Welfare Scheme: Boarding House Stipend to ST boarders of Govt. Hostel Description: ST students who are residing in Govt. boarding house will be benefitted with Rs. 65 / day of presence. Students studying in Class I to Class XII are eligible. Workflow: 1. Maker – Hostel Superintendent 2. Checker – respective SDWO 3. Approver – TW Department Pilot run will be launched in Assisted mode only where Hostel Superintendent will be filling up the applicants’ details as Maker. Payment
