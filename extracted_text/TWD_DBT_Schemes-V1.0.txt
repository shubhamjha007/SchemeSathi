A) Scheme: Pre-Matric scholarship to ST Students (Class VI-VIII) Eligibility (as per guidelines): i) Belongs to ST community a. Applicant / Father ST certificate OR b. Self-declaration by Father; authenticated by Headmaster OR c. Applicant / Father name in RC with Tribal surname OR d. Applicant / Father PRTC with Tribal surname ii) Not getting any other centrally funded pre-matric scholarship. iii) Not availing boarding house stipend (taken from previous guidelines). iv) Full time student studying in Govt. / Govt. aided / Govt. recognised (including private schools recognised by Central / State boards). Fully funded schools are not included. v) If studying in private school, family income bar is Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum. a. Family income certificate only for private school Benefits: As per guidelines, Rs. 40 / month / student for 10 months (Rs.40x10=Rs.400/-). In practice, payment is done once in a year for entire Rs. 400/-. Workflow issued by Dept.: i) Maker: Student or SDWO ii) Checker: DWO iii) Approver: TW Department Supporting Documents: 1. As BMS is ration card based system, only (i)(c) clause above may be considered. System will capture the RC no. and map beneficiary with RC member ID. No need to upload document. 2. May fetch income certificate from e-District application when integrated. No need to upload document. Otherwise, income certificate to be uploaded only for Private schools. 3. A declaration certified by the headmaster stating that he/she is not availing boarding house stipend or any other centrally funded pre-matric scholarship in the present year. In case of G2G mode, school wise list will be collected from headmaster and uploaded by SDWO. District, category wise list of schools is provided. Online Form: Sl. No. Field Sl.
