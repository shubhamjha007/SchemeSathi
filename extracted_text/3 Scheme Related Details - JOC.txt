A) Scheme Name: Coaching to ST students/ youth for appearing in the various job oriented competitive examination (viz. TCS/ TPS/ IAS/ Bank Service/ Railway etc) a. Scheme Details: The scheme have been launched for imparting Free Job Oriented Coaching to ST aspirants for appearing in various competitive examinations i.e. Combined Course for SSC/ Railway/ IBPS/ TPSC Combined Examination, Combined Course for JRBT and Other Gr -C & Gr-D recruitment Examination and Combined Course for CTET, STPGT, STGT, TET -II, TET -I and other teaching related recruitment Examination. b. Benefit details: Institutes will be empanelled for which separate tender will be floated following DFPRT regulations. Students will apply online selecting the empanelled institutes of their choice with the preferences. The course fee against the finally admitted students in the concerned empanelled institutes is paid to the head of the institutions in 2 instalments i.e. 1st inst. in the beginning of the course starting date and final inst. towards the end of the course duration.
