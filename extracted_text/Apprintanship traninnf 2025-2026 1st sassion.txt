Scheme: Apprenticeship training for pass out Engineering (Degree / Diploma) candidates of Civil OR Mechanical OR Electrical Engineering under PWD (R&B) (FY 2025-26, 1st Session). Steps: - 1. Visit https://bms.tripura.gov.in 2. Click on Citizen Login. 3. First time user needs to be register by clicking “Register here” link. 4. After registration, again go to Citizen login and provide mobile no. / email id. An OTP will be received. Enter the OTP will and Submit. 5. Under “Others” tab, the scheme will be listed. Click on enroll, fill-up the online form and submit. 6. Acknowledgement and status can be found under Dashboard menu available on top horizontal menu bar.
