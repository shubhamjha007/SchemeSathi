BRIEF SUMMARY FOR ONBOARDING SCHEMES OF MINORITIES WELFARE DEPARTMENT A) Scheme: Pre-Matric Scholarship for Minorities. Eligibility (as per guidelines): i) Belongs to Minorities Community ii) Minority Students from Class VI to Class X. iii) Income maximum limit 0.60 Lakhs.
